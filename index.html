About Me

I am a Lecturer in the School of Mathematics and Statistics at Shaanxi Normal University.

I received my Ph.D. in Operations Research and Optimization Control from the Department of Applied Mathematics at The Hong Kong Polytechnic University in 2025, supervised by Prof. Defeng Sun and Prof. Binyan Jiang. I was a Postdoctoral Fellow in the Department of Applied Mathematics at The Hong Kong Polytechnic University from June 2025 to June 2026. I joined the School of Mathematics and Statistics at Shaanxi Normal University as a Lecturer in July 2026.

Research Interests

  • High-Dimensional Optimization — efficient algorithms for sparse and structured sparse optimization problems.
  • Machine Learning — optimization-based methods for high-dimensional and structured learning problems.
  • High-Dimensional Statistical Optimization — sparse precision matrix estimation and related structured estimation problems.
