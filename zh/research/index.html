Research

My research focuses on high-dimensional optimization, machine learning, and high-dimensional statistical optimization, with an emphasis on efficient numerical algorithms and computational methods for large-scale and high-dimensional learning problems.

Research Interests

High-Dimensional Optimization

Efficient algorithms for high-dimensional optimization problems involving sparse and structured sparse solutions.

Sparse Optimization Structured Sparsity Large-Scale

Machine Learning

Optimization-based methods for machine learning and statistical learning, particularly for high-dimensional and structured learning problems.

Statistical Learning High-Dimensional Structured Models

High-Dimensional Statistical Optimization

Optimization models arising in high-dimensional statistical estimation, including sparse precision matrix estimation and related structured estimation.

Precision Matrix Graphical Models Statistical Estimation
